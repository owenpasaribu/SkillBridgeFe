About Us

Bridging the Gap Between Education and the Future of Work

SkillBridge was born from one simple problem: students learn a lot of things, but rarely know whether what they are learning is enough for the career they are aiming for.

Why Not a Job Portal?

A job portal helps you find openings. SkillBridge helps you find out whether you are ready for those openings — and if not, what you need to learn. We deliberately did not build a job search feature, so the product stays focused on readiness and skill development.

How It Works

SkillBridge compares the skills you have with the skills your target career requires, then builds a personalized learning roadmap from the gaps it finds. Everything is data-driven — not guesswork.
