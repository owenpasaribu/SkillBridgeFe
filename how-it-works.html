How It Works

From not knowing where to start to having a clear learning plan

1

Choose Your Career

Choose the career you are aiming for from the available list — from Data Analyst to Product Manager.

2

Assess Your Skills

Answer scenario-based questions for each relevant skill, so the result is more objective than simply ticking boxes.

3

Discover Your Skill Gap

See how your skill levels compare with the required standard, with categories and the reason behind each priority.

4

Get Your Roadmap

Get a step-by-step learning plan generated automatically from your gaps, with resources and a mini project for each phase.

5

Track Your Growth

Track your Career Readiness Score and skill progress over time on the My Growth page.
